Oven Demo - Oven Edge (Client)

FactoryTalk Optix 1.8 | MQTT | RecipeX | SQLite | ECharts | OPC UA | 6 languages | Light/Dark theme

A simulated pizza oven with its own operator HMI. It runs a realistic bake cycle (PreHeat, Bake, Cooldown), shows temperature, energy and alarms, keeps a local recipe book and can join a plant-wide supervisor with a single button: it publishes its telemetry, receives approved recipes, and chats with the head office.

It is the "machine" half of the OvenDemo NextGen pair. The "head office" half is the Optix_Demo_OvenDemo_Server project (see its OvenDemo_Server_Overview.md).

An oven works on its own as well: the simulation, the recipes, the alarms and the dashboard do not depend on the supervisor.

2. Demo walkthrough (about 3-5 minutes)

  1. Dashboard - the oven card shows state, current phase, temperature and setpoint, applied recipe, batch, uptime, cycles today and power. A live chart traces the actual temperature of the running cycle against the setpoint.
  2. Recipes - open the library. Approved recipes can be applied; applying while a cycle is running takes effect at the end of the cycle.
  3. Start / Stop - one button starts an idle oven and stops a running one. Watch the heater cycle on and off around the setpoint, then the oven cool down.
  4. Make something go wrong - flip Reject next cycle (the finished batch counts as rejected and lowers the quality figure the supervisor computes) or Put pineapples on pizza (raises an alarm).
  5. Alarms - active list and history, with priority filter.
  6. Settings - the oven has an id like OVEN-1A2B3, generated at first start. Set the supervisor address and press Request onboarding. The status goes Pending -> Onboarded once the supervisor accepts.
  7. Chat - answer the supervisor's message; delivery status updates on both screens.
  8. Offboarding - Force offboarding resets the onboarding status on the oven side (useful when the oven was removed on the supervisor); the oven keeps working stand-alone.

Functions in detail

  • Process simulation - three phases per recipe, each with temperature setpoint, duration, fan-in / fan-out ramps and a heating flag. The heater switches on and off around the setpoint with a dead band; the oven cools towards ambient when not heating. Simulated power is a 12 kW heater plus fans. The cycle ends automatically after the last phase.
  • Recipe-driven behavior - the phases are read from the recipe target every tick, so applying a recipe changes the oven from the next tick on. Recipes are transferred with RecipeX (TransferFromStoreToTarget).
  • Production counters - cycles, good / rejected cycles, energy, running time and ideal production time, with a counter epoch so the supervisor can tell a restart from a counter reset. They feed the supervisor's OEE page.
  • Alarms - four simulated process alarms ("Pineapples on pizza", "Mozzarella being finished", "Pizza is being overcooked", "Pizza is ready") with auto-acknowledge. The pineapple alarm is driven by a dashboard switch, the others by the alarm simulation logic. The active alarm count is part of the telemetry.
  • Onboarding - on the first start the oven generates its own id (OVEN- + 5 hex digits, kept in retentive storage). The onboarding request carries the id and the OPC UA endpoint of the oven; the supervisor answers with a status: none, pending, onboarded, declined or timeout.
  • Telemetry - once onboarded, the oven publishes its OvenType snapshot periodically on the MQTT topic named after its id.
  • Recipes from the supervisor - approved recipes arrive on recipes/<id>/to-edge, are validated (only Approved or Deprecated are accepted), stored read-only, acknowledged, and announced in a catalog message whenever the oven reconnects so that missing recipes are sent again. The oven reports back which recipe it applied.
  • Chat - messages to the supervisor are queued while offline and sent on reconnect; delivery is confirmed by the supervisor's acknowledgement; duplicates are ignored; history is capped per conversation.
  • Localization and themes - English, Spanish, Italian, French, German and Chinese; Light and Dark.

MQTT topics used by the oven

Topic Direction Content
OnboardingRequest oven -> supervisor {Sender, Content (OPC UA endpoint), Timestamp}
OnboardingResult supervisor -> oven Onboarding status (0 none, 1 pending, 2 onboarded, 3 declined, 4 timeout)
<DeviceId> oven -> supervisor Telemetry (Optix Default JSON of OvenType)
chat/<DeviceId>/to-edge, chat/<DeviceId>/to-supervisor both Chat message / acknowledgement (QoS 1)

The MQTT client id of the oven is its DeviceId.

5. Run it

Prerequisites: FactoryTalk Optix Studio 1.8.0.555 or later.

  1. Open Optix_Demo_OvenDemo_Client.optix in Studio and start it (Emulator). The WebUI is on http://<ip>:8080.
  2. Apply an Approved recipe in Recipes and press Start on the Dashboard.
  3. To join a fleet: open Settings, set the supervisor's IP address, press Request onboarding and accept the request on the Optix_Demo_OvenDemo_Server (Device registry).

Running several clients on the same PC requires changing their ports (WebUI 8080, OPC UA 59101); on separate devices no change is needed. Each oven needs its own id, generated automatically at first start.

6. Good to know

  • Recipes are created and approved on the supervisor. The oven only receives, stores and applies them. A recipe that is not Approved cannot be applied.
  • The demo link to the supervisor is anonymous and not encrypted (trade-show LAN). Production use needs TLS and authentication.
  • Translations cover the main labels in six languages; some texts of this project are still English only, the supervisor project is the fully localized one.
